**Bugs found and fixed during this check**

1. `change_log::feed` returned `cursor: 0` on an empty poll, so the page's
   live cursor reset and the next batch was swallowed by the first-poll
   guard — no pop-up ever showed. Fixed: empty feed keeps `after`
   (test `feed_groups_changes_into_one_batch_per_run`).
2. A part with no customer rendered as `" 100%"` in change text. Fixed:
   reads "Unresolved 100%" (test `a_block_with_no_customer_reads_unresolved`).

**Known limit (not fixed):** the catch-up loads once on mount; if the
daemon is down at that moment the chip stays hidden until the next page
load.
